01 / How I think

The happy path
is only the start.

I’m Md Yusuf Ahmed—a QA Automation Engineer who connects product risk to useful test coverage.

I work from the intent of a feature through what can go wrong: explore the experience, validate the API and data behind it, then automate checks that help a team ship with confidence. For AI features, that also means asking whether responses stay accurate, on-topic and safe.

↳ Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh (UTC+6) · Open to remote SDET and AI Quality roles
